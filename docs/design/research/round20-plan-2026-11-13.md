## Success criteria (for synthesis only, never in a brief)

- Five-second read: about how sure you are, whether your gut is right; not a to-do list or a
  quiz app.
- Fresh installs reach the Warm-up's result; L1–L2 runs don't leave before it.
- A first real prediction from their own life, in under a minute, filed where they'd look for
  it.
- The share or tell task: finds the card, can say what it shows; for a fresh install the
  Day-0 card; Mateo can compare his with the friend's text.
- Keep or pay: finds Plus, can say what it adds (the Coach, trends, cosmetics) and that the
  core is free.
- Findability tasks: the reminder's time and words (Nadia), where they're most off (Katrin),
  the streak (Youssef), how the score works (Somchai).
- Recovery tasks: a wrong answer changed (Somchai, Youssef), a wrong category or words fixed
  (Callum, Rim, Lakshmi), a prediction deleted (Mateo).
- Comprehension: the score is about confidence matching outcomes, not being right; nothing
  counts until predictions are answered; 20 answers before a rating; same-day answers aren't
  scored (where met).
- Step 176 in passing: the reminder shows no words by default (Nadia's privacy question); the
  streak-ended line (Youssef, if his streak ended within the week); "Save and log another"
  keeps the category; the Can't tell note.
