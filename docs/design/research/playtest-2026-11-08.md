# Blind playtests (November 2026, seventh round: UI limits)

## Method

Same as `playtest-2026-11-04.md`: each run was a fresh agent with only the
Playwright browser tools, no repo and no docs, given a persona, tasks and a fixed
report format, one after another in one browser. Run 1 started from a cleared
install (SQLite's OPFS file removed from a same-origin static page). Runs 2–5
used the demo data, loaded through `/dev/seed` on top of run 1's predictions,
each keeping what the run before left (run 3 added nine oddly named
categories, which runs 4–5 saw). Screenshots are in
`.playwright-mcp/playtest-2026-11-08/` (gitignored). No run signed in, bought
anything, erased data or left localhost.

Stand-ins: large text through CSS `zoom` 2 and 1.5 (run 2). Zoom also halves
the layout width (390 → 195 CSS px), harsher than iOS's largest Dynamic Type
size, which keeps the width; the failures it shows are the same kind.
Screen reader through the accessibility tree, keyboard only (run 5).

**Web-only, so not ranked below** (checked against the code): Log's × drops a
draft without asking (iOS asks, `useLogDraftGuard`; web has no action sheet);
the "+" is 32 px (44 pt with `hitSlop` on iOS); the confidence slider can't take
keyboard focus (it is one VoiceOver adjustable on iOS); switch descriptions
not read (iOS reads them as the hint); the switch is 40 × 20 with a dark thumb
lost on the card (iOS draws its own 63 × 28 switch, seen on the device);
visited tabs stay in the Tab order under `aria-hidden`, sheets drop focus to
`body`, Escape closes nothing, every route titled "Fairly Sure", every heading
`h1`; the lightning-bolt button (Expo's dev overlay).

| # | Persona | Start | Screen | Verdict | Score |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Dana, 31, QA engineer, breaks inputs for a living | Fresh install | 320 × 568 and 568 × 320, light | "It let me file a prediction due in the year 9999 and showed it as 'Fri, Dec 31' everywhere" | 7/10 |
| 2 | Walt, 67, retired bookkeeper, macular degeneration, largest text | Demo data | 390 × 844, dark, zoom 2 and 1.5 | "Half the screens break apart letter by letter, and the chart turns into a pile of overlapping numbers" | 4/10 (7 at 100%) |
| 3 | Rosa, 24, social-media manager, many jokey emoji categories | Run 2's data | 375 × 812, 320 × 568, 812 × 375, light | "One long category name breaks four screens, and the recap posts my worst category" | 6/10 |
| 4 | Ines, 38, design lead, two top-100 iOS apps | Run 3's data | 390 × 844, light then dark | "Three gutters, five sheet-header patterns, six ways to show selected and four words for answered" | 6/10 |
| 5 | Kofi, 45, blind, accessibility consultant, keyboard and screen reader | Run 4's data | 390 × 844, light, Reduce Motion | "Labels better than most I audit; focus and announcements send it back" | 4/10 (mostly web-only) |

**What held up.** Double-tapping Save or Yes never doubled anything. Whitespace
titles can't be saved and the hint says why; pasted line breaks become spaces;
an 80-character word breaks inside itself; emoji, ZWJ emoji, Arabic and Hebrew
render and align right. 40 rapid tab switches and fast sheet open/close left
nothing stuck. Duplicate category names are blocked whatever the case. Every
figure reconciled across Today, Insights, History, Resolve and the card (147
resolved = bucket sum = category sum; run 2). Resolve reflows cleanly at
200%. The chart's text alternative, card labels and badge-row labels are rich.
One typeface and the ink-only palette held everywhere; orange and blue mean only
over and under. Practice and Resolve scored 8/10 with the design lead.

## Ranked findings (iPhone-relevant)

### P1: breaks at the limits

1. **Long category names aren't bounded anywhere** (runs 3, 4, 5). A 24-character
   wide name runs out of its row in Categories (to x 444 on 376), off the right of
   Log's and Edit's chip row (Edit then scrolls sideways 16 px), *under* the
   "Getting started" pill on Insights (overlap), past the card edge in Today and
   History meta lines (x 386 on a 375 card) and the details label; the History
   filter chip is clipped both sides. One line with an ellipsis, and a fixed slot
   for the badge pill.
2. **Text beside a fixed-width item gets squeezed at large text** (run 2). Today's
   practice row (icon + "Start" + chevron) leaves the text about 18 px: "T / o / d
   / ay". Insights' badge rows squeeze "Score 87, give or take 10" to "Scor / e
   87". Switch rows push the main switch past the card edge. Stack the item under
   the text when the text is large.
3. **Categories rows wrap at 320 wide** (run 3): the switch drops to its own line,
   left-aligned under the icon, even for "x" or "Social". The name should shrink,
   not the row.
4. **The chart at large text** (run 2): labels are a fixed 12 px in a chart pinned
   to the column width; "n=52" lands on "Underconfident", the x-axis reads
   "80100". The range buttons truncate the headline bucket to "80–10…".
5. **The grey luck bars are about 1.3:1 in dark mode** (runs 2, 4), and the caption
   asks you to judge every dot by them. Already step 158 (D47).
6. **Dates far ahead** (run 1): Pick a date accepts 12/31/9999, and no label shows a
   year ("Fri, Dec 31", which is a Thursday this year). Cap the picker (a year or
   two) and show the year whenever it isn't this year. The demo's Dec 31 2027
   item already reads with a wrong weekday (run 2).
7. **History's list is a scroller inside the page** (runs 1, 2, 4): under fixed
   filters it is 84 px tall in landscape and 168 px at 200%. Let the filters and
   summary scroll away with the list.
8. **Your card in landscape** (run 3): the preview scales to the width, 757 × 1009
   on a 375-tall screen; tapping Story leaves it off-screen. Cap it to the height.
9. **The weekly digest's day pills** overflow at large text ("SuMorTueWeThuFriSat",
   run 2) and touch at 100% (1 px apart, run 4).

### P1: hierarchy and story

10. **Insights buries its point** (run 4): the 88 is followed by five 13 px grey
    lines (give-or-take, week change, Brier, same-day note, totals) before "You're
    overconfident at 80–100%" at y 406. Bucket counts appear three times (chart
    n=, range boxes, counts table); the Plus card (~460 px) sits between the badges
    and "Share my card".
11. **The 70 / 85 / 90 meter has no label** on Today and Insights (runs 2, 4, 5): it
    reads as raw numbers, unclear whose score, and VoiceOver hears "70, 85, 90".
12. **The recaps lead with the weakest category** (runs 3, 4): the only tier line is
    "Tracker in finance: 3 to go"; "Sharp in health" never appears, and the year
    card leads with a count. As a post it advertises the failure.
13. **Log with many categories** (runs 3, 4): 13 chips push Confidence to y 557 and
    Due date to 695 of 844.

### P2: share card polish (run 3 would not post it yet)

14. Chips sit on the footer divider with 0 px gap (Post at 320, Story); Story has
    ~50 px dead under the footer instead of using 9:16.
15. The footer's left edge differs from the body's (27 px in run 3's Post, 4 px in
    run 4's).
16. The five-dot row has no label (step 158, D47); chip text is ~10 px at story size.
17. Share buttons sit above Shape, On the card and Theme: share before you
    configure. Week and Year have no "Share as text" and no Shape.
18. "N categories not shown" is on the image (D42, decided: keep, but give it room).
19. Locked themes open the paywall with no preview.

### P2: limits said nowhere

20. Silent truncation: the title at 200 characters ("…chargebacks, and"), the
    reflection at 500, a category name at 24 (an emoji dropped, a trailing space
    kept). A counter near the limit.
21. Controls locked without a reason: the last shown category's switch looks on
    and active; the last card category can't be unticked, silently; hidden
    category rows aren't dimmed.
22. Categories' switch has no visible label (explained only in the footnote);
    13 identical Rename pills.

### P2: consistency (run 4's inventory)

23. **Gutters:** 16 px (tabs, Log, details, Practice), 20 (Your card), 24 (Paywall,
    Sign in).
24. **Sheet headers, five patterns:** title in line with ×, title under a floating ×,
    28 px title, 34 px title, no title. **Dismissal, four:** × only; × + Cancel
    (Edit); × + Done (Resolve after); × + "Not now" (Paywall). Edit's Save scrolls
    with the form; Log's is pinned.
25. **Selection, six treatments:** ink chips, ink chips with checks, grey radio
    cards, ink 10 px tiles, circular day pills, segmented control. Practice's
    picked option is grey, unlike ink everywhere else. In dark mode the selected
    segment is darker than its track (1.1:1).
26. **Same action, two looks:** "Change to: it didn't happen" is a text link in
    Resolve and an outlined button in details; "Answer it now" link vs "Answer
    now" filled.
27. **Terms:** resolved (History rows, Insights), answered (History counter,
    details), scored (week recap); calls / predictions / things. Insights' 148
    resolved vs History's 149 answered on the same day: the one unscored same-day
    answer, said nowhere.
28. **Formats:** "Sun, Nov 8", "Sunday, Nov 15", "Nov 8", "Fri, Dec 31"; "7:00 PM" in
    copy beside "07:00 PM" in the field ("19:00" on the device); "Until 12:47 /
    AM" and "Nov / 8" break across lines (need non-breaking spaces).
29. **Type:** Insights alone uses 48, 22, 20, 17, 15, 13, 12, 11 px; section
    headings are 17/600 on one screen and 20/600 on another.

### P2–P3: structure

30. How scoring works is 3,346 px of 17 px prose with no summary or collapsing.
31. A resolved prediction's details open the whole category picker inline.
32. The paywall's "Start 1 month free" is below the fold; "free" said three times.
33. "Answer it now" repeats the due date just above it; 8 px to its button.
34. History filter chips are 36 px (Log's 44); the row has no cue that it scrolls,
    and empty categories still get chips.
35. The warm-up's first question has its confidence below the fold at 320 and
    nothing says why Next is grey; the result's only button is at the top.
36. The Calibrating card says "0 of 20 resolved" then "20 more resolutions": the
    same fact twice.
37. The reflection box is a fixed four lines with its own scrollbar.

### Accessibility on iOS too

38. Resolve's "Recorded: it happened", "Change to…", Rename and Add aren't
    announced (Log's "Saved." is, `announceForAccessibility`).
39. "Can't tell / doesn't apply"'s line "It won't count toward your score." isn't
    in its label.
40. A History card's label carries the whole reflection (~1,000 characters).
41. Arabic and Hebrew titles have no language, so they're read in the English
    voice.
