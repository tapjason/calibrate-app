# The under-13 rule (D70): design and build spec

2026-11-19. `docs/SHIP_READINESS.md` decision 2. The owner delegated the call to the main
session ("apply your recommendations"); the design analysis below was done the same day
(read-only, sources at the end). **Not legal advice**: the points under "For counsel" are
the owner's to take to a lawyer before the public US release.

## The decision

When Apple's Declared Age Range says under 13, the app is **local-only**: no sign-in, no
sync, no usage counts, no Coach, no RevenueCat, and Plus isn't offered. Everything else
works as usual. One predicate decides it, one store field carries it, and every upload path
reads that field.

Why: the app already asks the question (the Texas, Utah and Louisiana app-store acts tell
developers to use the store's signal, and Apple says "you must check the age" where
required). Holding a signal that someone is a child while still collecting from them is the
worst position under COPPA's "actual knowledge" standard. Data that "is stored on the device
and is never transmitted" is not collection (FTC COPPA FAQ F.5), so local-only removes the
question. Guideline 5.1.4(a) asks that an app stay useful "regardless of a person's age",
which the free core loop is.

Not adopted: keeping purchases (a third party would get a per-install id for a known child,
and Plus without the Coach is thin); removing the question (leaves the state acts' "verify"
duty unmet); keeping the id-less guest counts ("stays on this phone" would stop being
plainly true); blocking the app (5.1.4(a)).

## The main session's calls (2026-11-19), each for the owner or counsel to overturn

1. **The predicate is tolerant of both readings of Apple's bounds.** Apple's sandbox table
   says under 13 comes back as `(lowerBound: nil, upperBound: 12)` (inclusive); Apple's
   `upperBound` reference page reads as if it would be 13. So:
   `isUnder13Band(b)` is true when `b.upperBound !== null && b.upperBound <= 13 &&
   (b.lowerBound === null || b.lowerBound < 13)`. True for (null, 12), (null, 11), (9, 12),
   (null, 13), (12, 13). False for (13, 15), (13, 16), (16, 17), (18, null), (null, 15),
   (null, 17), null. A band that may include a 13-year-old only because a region's gate
   sits at 14 is treated as under 13: the band includes children under 13, and the rule
   fails closed. The owner's sandbox check confirms which reading is real; the rule fires
   either way.
2. **RevenueCat and the guest flush wait for Apple's answer** on a launch that will ask
   (iOS 26, with no stored band or a stored under-13 band). Otherwise a child's first launch
   makes an anonymous RevenueCat record before the band arrives. A paywall open or a sign-in
   waits at most 5 s for the answer, then goes on as today (the sheet is modal, so a child
   can't reach either while it is up). Everywhere the question isn't asked (web, Jest,
   Android, iOS before 26, a stored band that isn't under 13) nothing waits and nothing
   changes.
3. **An account already signed in is frozen, not signed out and not deleted.** Uploads and
   the session's refresh stop; the rows stay on the phone; Sign out and Delete account still
   work (Delete account is the one upload path left open: it removes data). The account may
   be an adult's on a child's phone, and deleting someone's data is the owner's call.
4. **Erase and account deletion still clear the band**, as promised. The gate stays on in
   memory for the rest of that run, and the next launch asks Apple again before anything can
   be sent (Apple's cache answers without a prompt).
5. **While the stored band is under 13, the app asks again** once per launch and, in a
   long run, on return to the foreground once 24 hours have passed. Apple caches the answer
   and prompts only on the anniversary (or when the person shares again from Settings), so
   the repeat is silent. A different valid band replaces the stored one; a decline, "not
   available" or an error keeps it. Any other stored band is never asked again, as today.
6. **The rating prompt isn't requested** on a local-only phone.
7. **No band means today's behaviour**: declined, unavailable, older iOS, web, Jest.

## API facts (Apple, `expo-age-range` 0.2.21)

- Gates 13 / 16 / 18. Apple's sandbox table ("age ranges are inclusive"): under 13 is
  (nil, 12); 13–15 is (13, 15); 16–17 is (16, 17); 18+ is (18, nil). Regulated regions can
  replace the gates ("the returned age range reflects regulatory requirements rather than
  the bounds of your age gates"), so a lower bound can appear on an under-13 band.
- Repeat calls: cached per app. "When in Ask First, by default, it will only prompt on the
  anniversary of the original response"; Always shares silently; Never declines silently.
  "The API continues returning the previous range until the anniversary of their original
  declaration"; Settings → [name] → Personal Information → Age Range for Apps → the app →
  Share Age Range Again forces it sooner.
- Decline: expo throws `ERR_AGE_RANGE_USER_DECLINED`. Not available (no Apple Account, or
  not set up): `ERR_AGE_RANGE_NOT_AVAILABLE`. Before iOS 26 and on web expo returns a fake
  `lowerBound: 18`; the repo already refuses to call there.
- Other fields (`ageRangeDeclaration`, `activeParentalControls`) change nothing and are
  still not stored: an under-13 band is treated the same whoever declared it.
- `src/age/ageRange.test.ts` uses `(null, 13)` as its under-13 example; keep it and add
  `(null, 12)`.

## Every way data leaves the phone, and its gate

| Path | Carries | Under the rule |
|---|---|---|
| `src/supabase/auth.ts` sign-in and sign-up (Apple, Google, email) and the store actions over them | Email, Apple or Google id, session, IP | Refused in each service function and each store action |
| Session upkeep (`autoRefreshToken`, `getSession`) | Refresh token, IP | Auto-refresh stopped while the rule applies (residual: disclosed) |
| `src/supabase/sync.ts`: `syncNow`, `pushNow`, `backfillOffsets`, and a sweep in flight | Predictions under the user id | The entry points do nothing; the per-request "still current" check stops a sweep between requests |
| `src/analytics/track.ts`, `flush.ts`; `usageCountsMayGoUp` | Usage counts (account id when signed in; none for a guest; an IP either way) | Nothing is queued, nothing is sent; the queue is cleared on entering the rule and the usage-notice stamp forgotten |
| `src/ai/coach.ts`, `src/ai/refine.ts` (dormant) | JWT and computed numbers | Return the empty result before any client is built |
| `src/supabase/account.ts` delete-account, Apple re-auth for deletion, sign-out | JWT, Apple code | **Stay allowed**: they remove data |
| `src/billing/`: RevenueCat configure at launch (a guest gets a per-install anonymous id), entitlement refresh, plans, purchase, restore | Per-install id or the account id, IP, receipts | Never configured; billing reads as unavailable; Plus and the paywall aren't offered |
| App Store link in a text share; links opened in Safari's sheet; Apple's own age call | No id from the app | Unchanged |
| `src/review/ratingPrompt.ts` | Nothing from the app | Not requested |

No `expo-updates`; fonts are embedded.

## Build spec

**The one predicate:** `isUnder13Band` (pure, `src/engine/ageBand.ts`). **The one field:**
`useAgeStore.localOnly`, read through `isLocalOnly()`. **For automatic uploads at launch:**
`uploadsMayStart()` = `settled && !localOnly`.

### Core (types, db, engine, stores)

- `src/types/index.ts`: `export const LOCAL_ONLY_BELOW_AGE = 13;` and `AgeBucket`'s comment.
- `src/engine/ageBand.ts` (new): `isUnder13Band(bucket: AgeBucket | null | undefined): boolean`.
- `src/store/ageStore.ts` (new; a leaf: zustand, `@/db/ageBucket`, `@/engine/ageBand`,
  `@/types` only, so stores and services can both read it without a cycle):
  - state `{ bucket: AgeBucket | null; localOnly: boolean; settled: boolean }`, default
    `{ null, false, true }`;
  - `load(bucket, opts: { willAsk: boolean })`: sets the bucket, `localOnly =
    isUnder13Band(bucket)`, `settled = !willAsk`;
  - `accept(bucket: AgeBucket): Promise<void>`: saves it, sets `localOnly` from it;
  - `markSettled()`;
  - `forgetBand()`: bucket to null, `localOnly` left as it is for this run;
  - helpers `isLocalOnly()`, `uploadsMayStart()`, `whenAgeSettled(maxWaitMs?: number):
    Promise<void>` (resolves at once when settled, on `markSettled`, or at the cap).
- `src/store/authStore.ts`: `export const LOCAL_ONLY_SIGN_IN = 'Accounts are off on this
  phone because of the age range it shares with Fairly Sure.'`; every sign-in and sign-up
  action returns `{ ok: false, error: LOCAL_ONLY_SIGN_IN }` when local-only; guest Erase
  and a confirmed account deletion call `forgetBand()` after the wipe. Sign-out is as
  built (its last push sends nothing under the rule, so "Sign out anyway?" names what is
  unsynced). Delete account still calls the server.
- `src/store/ratingStore.ts`: no request when `isLocalOnly()`.
- No SQLite migration: 023 stays the latest.
- Tests: the predicate's table above; the store's defaults, a lift on a 13+ band,
  `forgetBand` keeping `localOnly`, `whenAgeSettled` on `markSettled` and at its cap; an
  auth test that **enumerates** the store's `signIn*` / `signUp*` actions and asserts each
  refuses with a client whose every property access throws (so a new action is covered
  without editing the test); a stored session under the rule stays signed in and sends
  nothing; a band arriving mid-session wipes nothing; `deleteAccount` still reaches the
  server.

### Services

- `src/age/ageRange.ts`: `hydrateAgeGate(): Promise<void>` reads the stored band (an error
  reads as none) and calls `load(bucket, { willAsk: deps.isAvailable() && (bucket === null
  || isUnder13Band(bucket)) })`; `ensureAgeBucket()` also asks when the stored band is
  under 13, keeps the stored band on anything but a valid band, calls `accept`, and always
  ends in `markSettled()`; `recheckAgeBucketIfDue(now)` for the foreground, at most once per
  `AGE_RECHECK_MIN_MS` (24 h, in memory). The header comment changes: the band now gates
  uploads.
- `src/supabase/auth.ts`: `refuseWhenLocalOnly()` (awaits `whenAgeSettled(5000)`), first
  statement of every sign-in and sign-up function; pause and resume of the session's
  auto-refresh.
- `src/supabase/sync.ts`: `syncNow`, `pushNow`, `backfillOffsets` do nothing when
  `isLocalOnly()`; the session check used between requests returns false too.
- `src/analytics/track.ts`, `flush.ts`: nothing queued when local-only; nothing sent unless
  `uploadsMayStart()`.
- `src/ai/coach.ts`, `refine.ts`: the empty result when `isLocalOnly()`, before a client.
- `src/billing/revenuecat.ts`: `apiKeyForPlatform()` returns `''` when `isLocalOnly()`
  (every function already treats that as unavailable). `src/billing/init.ts`: after the
  mirror hydrates, `await whenAgeSettled()`, configure only if not local-only, configure
  once on a lift; `ensureBillingStarted(maxWaitMs = 5000)` for the paywall.
- `src/age/localOnly.ts` (new): `initLocalOnly()`. On entering the rule: clear the queued
  events, forget the usage-notice stamp, stop the session's auto-refresh, reset the Coach.
  On leaving: resume auto-refresh, sync if signed in, flush if allowed.
- `src/supabase/account.ts`: no gate; a comment naming it as the permitted exception.
- Tests: `ensureAgeBucket` re-asks only for a stored under-13 band, once per process; a 13+
  answer lifts; a decline, a throw or an invalid band keeps it; a stored adult is never
  asked; web, Android and iOS 18 are settled at once. One test per gated module with the
  throwing client. Billing: `configure` never called when local-only, called after
  `markSettled` otherwise. **The guard, `src/age/egress.test.ts`:** scan non-test files
  under `src/` and `app/` for network calls (`fetch(`, `functions.invoke(`, `.from('`,
  `.rpc(`, `.auth.signIn`, `.auth.signUp`, `exchangeCodeForSession`, `verifyOtp`,
  `resetPasswordForEmail`, `updateUser`, `Purchases.`, `openAuthSessionAsync`,
  `XMLHttpRequest`, `WebSocket`); assert the files found equal an allow-list; assert each
  but `account.ts` reads the age store; assert every exported `sign(In|Up)*` function in
  `auth.ts` calls `refuseWhenLocalOnly(`. A new upload file or sign-in route fails the test
  until it is listed and gated.

### Screens

- `app/_layout.tsx`: `await hydrateAgeGate()` right after the database opens and before the
  auth store initialises; `initLocalOnly()`; `recheckAgeBucketIfDue` in the foreground
  listener.
- `src/components/age/localOnlyCopy.ts` (new): every string below, exported. A test asserts
  none of them matches `/\d|under|child|kid|young/i`: the app never says an age more
  precisely than "the age range".
- You (`SettingsView.tsx`): the account group shows the static explanation for a guest and
  the frozen footer when signed in; the Plus row, "Coach (AI)" and "Usage stats" are hidden.
- Today: no backup nudge. Insights: no Plus card; the Coach line only where a Plus mirror
  is active. The paywall and account routes render the local-only page and never mount
  `PaywallView` or `SignInView` (both are reachable by deep link); otherwise the paywall
  awaits `ensureBillingStarted()`.
- Warm-up: the usage line isn't drawn, and its stamp is written only when `settled &&
  !localOnly` (and unlocked, as today). Share: free themes only, no upgrade control.
- Unchanged: the app lock, reminders and the digest, share cards and Wrapped, CSV export,
  practice, the Warm-up itself, category icons, Trends on an existing Plus mirror.

## For counsel (not settled here)

1. Whether Apple's declared band is COPPA "actual knowledge" (the rule assumes yes).
2. Whether what a frozen account already has on the server must be deleted, and how fast.
3. Whether asking once and carrying on after a decline meets the state acts' "verify".
4. The significant-change notices and consent-revocation handling the acts and Apple
   describe; the app has neither.
5. Texas's delete-after-verification clause against a band kept on the phone.
6. Minors' subscriptions (13 to 17) under the Texas contract clause.
7. Under-16 consent ages outside the US, before any EU launch.

## What only the iPhone can show

- Whether under 13 comes back as 12 or 13 (the predicate takes both). Sandbox: Developer →
  Sandbox Apple Account → Manage → Age Assurance → "Under 13", Erase, relaunch; needs
  Developer Mode, a Sandbox Apple Account and iOS 26.2 or later.
- Whether the repeat ask is silent, and whether a decline is asked again at each launch.
- A request that never resolves leaves billing and the guest flush waiting for that run
  (the 5 s caps cover a paywall open and a sign-in).
- `expo-age-range` 0.2.21 asserts on an unknown declaration value (`confirmed` exists from
  iOS 26.5); a debug development build may trap there.
- Whether supabase-js refreshes a token inside `getSession()` after auto-refresh is stopped.
