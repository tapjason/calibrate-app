# Blind playtests (November 2026, ninth round: diversity and usability)

## Method

Each blind run was a fresh agent with only the Playwright browser tools, no repo
and no docs, given a persona, goals (never button or screen names), an
interruption, three end-of-run questions and the fixed report format (thinking
aloud with "expected / got", ease 1–7 per task, severity 0–4 per issue), one
after another in one browser. Before them, `web-playtester` ran the step 161
checks (all 11 passed) and a functional pass. Screenshots are in
`.playwright-mcp/playtest-2026-11-09/` (gitignored).

**The cast.** Chosen by §10's rule: none of the last two rounds' testers (Dana,
Walt, Rosa, Ines, Kofi, Priya); a fresh install and a returning user; two
second-language readers; four access needs other than large text; one run at
320 wide; no two runs on the same screen and appearance.

done after a detour or a loss.

**Expectation mismatches seen by two runs or more:** a half-typed prediction
gone after the app closed (1, 2, 6); no "Saved" after Save (1, 3; step 159 is
planned for it); the reminder sentence in AM/PM above a 24-hour field (2, 6;
and run 1 noticed "19:00" beside "7:00 PM"); Log not saying when the reminder
will come (1, 2, 6); the unlabelled 70/85/90 bar (2, 4); History's filter chips
running off the edge with no hint that they scroll (3, 5).

## Comprehension (answered from memory)

16 of 18 answers matched what the app means (`CLAUDE.md`).

- **Right:** what a stated percentage means (1); "give or take" (2, the same
  80% band the docs use); the score versus the badge, including "at its low end"
  once the worked example was read (2); the lean and its range (4, 5); the grey
  bars (4); what happens to a Can't tell (3, 5); when a score unlocks (6); what
  the card shows (4, 6); Save's requirements (3); when the reminders come (2, 6).
- **Partly right (finding 4):** how long a Yes or No can be changed (3: "it
  never told me a time limit"); what happened to the streak (5: "it just quietly
  started over").
- **Right, and a finding anyway:** run 1 read the Warm-up result correctly ("no
  clear lean" means it could be luck) and felt it "didn't really tell me anything
  about myself".

## What held up

- **Answering and correcting** are fast and trusted: three taps to answer,
  "Change to" right there, "Resolve all 38" cleared in four minutes, and nothing
  was lost to the interruptions in Resolve, History or the card (2, 3, 4, 5, 6).
- **The Can't tell rework from step 161** read as "the clearest screen in the
  app" (3) and "well thought out" (6): a stated deadline, and nothing written
  until an answer.
- **No-drag input:** the ±5 buttons set every confidence with no slider (3, 5,
  6).
- **Plain counts beat the score:** "Furthest off: Finance. You said about 85%,
  and 8 of 17 happened" (2, 4) and Log's "Answered 8 times before: happened 5 of
  8" (5) were named the most useful lines.
- **Under deuteranopia,** blue and yellow stay apart and every lean is also in
  words; the chart passes (4).
- **Kind wording where it counts:** the early-answer check, the delete
  confirmation that puts Cancel where a shaky second tap lands, "It isn't about
  being right" (1, 3).

## Ranked findings

"No decision" means the fix doesn't change what a screen says or how it's
arranged; "needs the owner" means it does, or touches a decided D number. Each
finding names the runs that hit it and the axis that exposed it.

### P1: what loses people

1. **A half-typed prediction is lost when the app closes** (1, 2, 6; the
   interruption, on the move). All three runs reloaded mid-Log and found a blank
   form. Severity 3 for Ingrid ("exactly when I would drop the app"), 2 for
   Grace, 1 for Mateus. Log keeps no draft; the guard only asks before a
   deliberate close. *Needs the owner:* keep an unsaved Log draft on the device
   (title, category, confidence, date) and restore it on the next open, with a
   way to discard.
2. **The due-day reminder ignores when the event happens, and Log doesn't say
   when it will ask** (2, 6; 1 wanted to be told). Mateus's 19:00 default lands
   mid-match for an 18:00 kick-off; Ingrid's 07:30 asks about a 09:00 run before
   it starts. Neither learns the time on Log. *Needs the owner:* at least say it
   under the date ("We'll ask on Sunday at 19:00, change in You"); possibly a
   time per prediction (D38 chose one time for all).
3. **Times ignore the phone's 24-hour setting** (2, 6; 1 noticed). Already
   finding 7 of `playtest-2026-11-08b.md`; now three second-language or
   24-hour runs. *Needs the owner:* `expo-localization` and a new build.
4. **What changing an answer allows isn't said where it's done** (3, and
   comprehension). Resolve's "Change to" gives no time limit; the details screen
   does ("until …"). Grace (1) couldn't change her confidence and got "it's the
   record" with no way forward (ease 2). *Needs the owner:* the window on Resolve
   ("You can change this until 20:00 tomorrow"); on Edit, a pointer to Delete and
   log it again, or a short grace period for the confidence (D25).
5. **A returning user isn't told what happened to the streak** (5; back after a
   gap, attention). With streak 0 the row is hidden (`streakCopy` returns null),
   it reappears as "1-day streak" with no word about the old one, and the row
   doesn't open the rules, which sit five screens down How scoring works. Ease 3.
   *Needs the owner:* a one-time "Your 23-day streak ended on Oct 18; one today
   starts a new one", and the row opening How scoring works at Streaks, as the
   identity line opens Badges (D27).

### P1: the record's story

6. **"Resolve all" makes Can't tell the fastest button** (5; attention). Yes or
   No costs two taps and repeats "In your 80–100% range, X of Y have happened",
   worse each time; Can't tell is one tap and silent. Jun: "made Can't tell the
   tempting fast button, which is the opposite of honest". The asymmetry was
   recorded as a speed-versus-undo trade on 2026-11-05. *Needs the owner.*
7. **Coming back after a gap reads as a punishment** (5). Clearing 38 honestly
   moved the rating 86 → 79 and Insights said "Down 7 since last week, from 28
   answers", all 28 answered that morning. Accurate (D37), but "since last week"
   misreads a backlog. *Needs the owner:* say "from 28 answers given today" when
   they were, or hold the weekly line until the answers span the week.
8. **The thing people share says the flattering half, or nothing** (4, 6; 1
   wouldn't post). The card carries tiers, not the lean; its five dots carry
   meaning by colour alone with no key (4: "decoration to anyone who isn't me";
   step 158 is planned for this); at day 6 it says only "Calibrating, 15 more"
   (6). The week recap speaks to the reader as "you" and ends on a to-do line
   meant for its owner (6), and its totals still include hidden categories while
   their titles are left off (6; titles are correctly suppressed,
   `WrappedCard.titleOf`). *Needs the owner* (D27, D42).

### P2: words (second-language and plain-language readers)

9. **The statistics vocabulary loses first-time and second-language readers**
   (1, 2, 4, 5). The Brier line ("Saying 65% every time, how often your calls
   came true, would score 0.23") was read three times by three runs; "calls"
   read as phone calls (2); "give or take", "at its low end", "pad", "flatter",
   "lean", "n=2", "resolved/answered" used for one count (6); the streak
   paragraph (2); the Warm-up result's chart and caption for a first-timer (1).
   *Needs the owner* (copy, several decided in D35).
10. **Unlabelled status:** the 70/85/90 bar (2, 4; already group 4 of
    2026-11-08), "Today's goal met" with three unlabelled dots (5; already a
    2026-11-05 point), the Calibrating box that looks tappable (1). *Needs the
    owner.*
11. **Insights hides "where am I off" under "Category badges", after a wall of
    numbers** (2, 5). *Needs the owner* (group 4 of 2026-11-08).

### P2: hands and eyes (iOS too)

12. **Small targets close together** (3; tremor). Rename and the hide switch
    4 pt apart on every Categories row (`CategoryManager` `controls`
    `columnGap: space.xs`), so a slip hides a category without a word; Edit and
    "Answer it now" 4 pt apart (`PredictionDetails` `actions` `gap: space.xs`);
    Done / Log it again / "Change to" 8 pt apart with "Change to" the smallest;
    the icon editor's Done 16 pt from "Use the default". *No decision:* 8–12 pt
    between neighbours. Whether hiding a category should say so is the owner's.
13. **Can't tell closes the sheet silently** (3, 5). No "Recorded" screen and no
    undo in sight, unlike Yes and No; the card just vanishes from Today. *Needs
    the owner* (2026-11-05 kept it).
14. **The recaps are white in dark mode** (4; dark room). Share cards keep fixed
    palettes by design (`CLAUDE.md`, DESIGN_SYSTEM). *Needs the owner.*
15. **History's filter strip gives no sign it scrolls** (3 at 440, 5 at 320).
    "Personal" cut at the edge, "Classroom" entirely off-screen. *No decision:*
    a fade at the trailing edge, or end the visible chips on a whole one.

### P3: polish

16. **A dangling comma when the date wraps under the category** on a card ("Work,"
    then "due Mon, Nov 9" below; 6). `PredictionCard` puts the comma inside the
    name's text. *No decision:* drop the comma when the two wrap, or set the date
    apart without one.
17. **"All caught up" counts only the run since the interruption** (5: 25 + 8,
    not 38). Minor. *Needs the owner* if the summary should be the day's.
18. **The Warm-up's first question is chemistry for everyone** (1: "my heart sank
    a little"). The ten are fixed (D18). *Needs the owner.*
19. **The Plus theme swatches are told apart only by colour; their names are for
    screen readers only** (4). *Needs the owner* (paywall copy).
20. **History has no outcome filter** ("only the misses"; 4). *Needs the owner*
    (new behaviour).
