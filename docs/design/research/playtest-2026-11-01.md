# Blind playtests (November 2026)

## Method

The same as the October dry runs (`dryrun-2026-10-09.md`): each run was a fresh
agent with only the Playwright browser tools, no repo and no docs, given a persona,
a goal and a fixed report format. They ran one after another, since they share a
browser. Runs 1 and 2 used a freshly cleared install; runs 3 to 6 used the demo
data (five months, about 150 answered). Screenshots are in
`.playwright-mcp/playtest/` (gitignored). None signed in, bought anything or erased
data. One opened its own screenshot file once; that was the only file read.

| 6 | Sam, 41, data scientist, judging rigour and gaming | Demo data | 375 × 812, light | **Trust partly**: "an honest journal, not a credential" | — |

Compared with October (two "delete", two "keep, barely"), every run kept the app.
The Day-0 complaint ("come back in a week") didn't recur: run 1 understood that the
first answer comes tomorrow evening.

Limits: agents can't feel haptics or see iOS sheets, and web differs from iOS in
keyboard focus, switches and file export. Page zoom stood in for Dynamic Type, which
scales text, not layout, so zoom overstates crowding. Runs 1 and 2 happened on one
real day, so "the next evening" had nothing due and the practice was already done.
The demo data was seeded weeks earlier, so its streak had lapsed ("1-day streak").

## What one run found that holds up

### Run 1 (first-timer)
- With 9 of 10 right at 78%, "On these ten, no clear lean" landed flat ("so… was I
  good?"). The rule is right (it's inside the 80% luck band); the line needs a
  plain read of what did happen.
- The best line in the app, "It isn't about being right. A 60% call that doesn't
  happen isn't a mistake", is two taps deep in How scoring works.
- Nothing says when the first real label could arrive.
- At 375 × 667 the Warmup's confidence control sits below the fold on question 1.

### Run 3 (power user)
- Finance is the real story (18 calls, all 80–100%, 8 happened, score 60), and no
  screen says so; she found it with History's filters. She wanted "In finance you
  say about 85% and it happens 44%. Try about 45%."
- The chart shows her underconfident at 60–80% (76% against 68%, n=55), but only
  overconfidence at 80–100% is named. She "compresses" and nothing says it.
- Nothing free shows change over time ("since last week").
- Plus shows no Coach sample on her own data, so she can't judge it.
- The second identity line changed from "Forecaster in personal" to "Forecaster in
  work" after answering (a tie at the same tier, broken by score), with no reason.
- "Boldest call that landed: 95%" beside "Surest thing that didn't: 95%" reads as a
  contradiction without titles.
- The oldest due prediction was four weeks old and nothing said so.

### Run 4 (accessibility)
- **Bug, confirmed in code:** under Reduce Motion, a disabled button looks enabled.
  `Button`'s press style sets `opacity: 1 - pressed * 0.2`, and it comes after the
  disabled style's `opacity: 0.4`, so it overrides it.
- A disabled Save doesn't say what's missing (a title, a number, a category).
- At 130% zoom, the coverage chips truncate ("20–4…"), words break mid-word ("Next
  mileston / e"), and History's list area shrinks to about one card. Zoom overstates
  Dynamic Type, but the truncated chips and split words point at real risks.
- The empty part of the slider's track is about 1.5:1.
- Web only (not iOS): hidden tabs stay in the keyboard Tab order (72 of 81 stops on
  Today, including Erase); sheets don't take focus or close on Escape; the slider
  can't be focused; Space doesn't pick a chip; every heading is level 1; answered
  rows show a hand cursor but do nothing. They matter if the web build becomes the
  web Warmup (FUTURE_UI A8).
- Already good: spoken labels as full sentences, the chart's text description,
  outcomes as icon and words, dark mode and Reduce Motion followed, focus rings.

### Run 5 (personaliser)
- **Bug, confirmed in code:** the card's shape and hidden categories reset every time
  the sheet opens (`ShareCardPanel` holds them in local state).
- Categories hidden on the card still appear on the weekly and yearly recaps.
- A locked theme opens the paywall with no preview of the theme.
- After picking a practice time, the chip still reads "Pick a time".
- With Notifications off, two rows disappear but the practice row stays, while the
  header says "Each kind has its own choice below".
- Wanted and missing: a default due date, Today's order, an in-app appearance
  setting. Never wanted: changing a confidence after saving ("the fixed record is
  the point").
- Web only: "Exporting isn't available on this device" (native uses the share sheet).

### Run 6 (analyst)
Every number he recomputed matched: the 87 rating (count-weighted), "give or take
4" (about one standard error), Brier 0.22, the grey bars, every "expected about N",
the yearly "expected about 105", badge thresholds, the warm-up and practice
arithmetic. What he found:
- **Padding:** sure things that happen add no miss and one to the count. Two "my
  paycheck lands this week" at 100%, answered Yes the same day, took finance from
  Guesser 60 to Tracker 64 (with a badge card) and the rating from 87 to 88. By his
  arithmetic about 6 such entries reach Forecaster and about 32 reach Sharp.
- **Selective reporting:** answer No, tap "Change answer", pick "Can't tell", and
  the miss leaves the score. A skip can be answered later, so the bad ones can be
  parked. The skip count shows only in History.
- **Brier baseline:** "always saying 50% scores 0.25" flatters; against his own base
  rate (64% of calls happened, so always saying 64% scores 0.230) his skill is about
  zero. The standard is a Brier skill score against the base rate.
- **Lean colours:** chart dots are coloured as a lean when they fall outside a 50%
  band, while the warm-up names a lean only outside an 80% band.
- **No give-or-take on category scores:** "Social 93, 17 resolved"; one answer moved
  it five points.
- **Claim drift:** an open prediction's title and due date can be edited after
  logging; the number stays fixed.
- He'd recommend it "as an honest journal; no, as a credential".

## Test artefacts, not app faults

- The square button in a bottom corner is the development build's tools menu.
- "1-day streak" after five months: the demo data was seeded in October.
- The practice was "already done" on the "next evening": same real day.
- CSV export and keyboard/focus behaviour differ on web (listed above).
- Screenshots of a direct page load sometimes showed the launch spinner: the test
  browser presents a stale frame when hidden (UI_ROADMAP §1).

## Fixes that needed no decision (built 2026-11-01, step 138)

1. Disabled buttons under Reduce Motion: the dimming moved into the press style, so
   the dip multiplies it instead of replacing it.
2. History's counts: the summary counts every answered row, as the heading does,
   skips included and named as not scored.
3. "N months on file": whole months since the first answer, and no number under one.
4. The card keeps its shape and hidden categories between visits.
5. The title field wraps and grows; Return is Done.
6. Edit shows the date field when "Pick a date" is already the choice.
7. The practice-time chip reads "Your time" over the chosen time.
8. The Coach switch, on without Plus, says it waits for Plus and sends nothing before.
9. Once the form is started, a line over a disabled Save says what it's waiting for.
10. The coverage chips' range labels break after the dash instead of truncating, and
    "Next milestone" starts its own line instead of breaking mid-word.

## Proposed: decisions for the owner

- **Fixing a recorded answer** (runs 2, 3, 5) together with **the skip hole** (run 6):
  for example, allow correcting an outcome or re-filing a category within a short
  window, deleting a test entry, and no "Can't tell" after a Yes/No; show the skip
  rate beside the rating.
- **Custom categories** (runs 1, 2, 3, 5): rename, add or hide. Changes the data
  model in `CLAUDE.md`.
- **Reminder times** (runs 1, 2, 3, 5): a time for due-day reminders, a day and time
  for the digest (D9 left "a user-set time" unbuilt).
- **Padding resistance** (run 6): badges need the score minus its give-or-take to
  clear the bar; a Brier skill score against the base rate; discount 95–100% calls or
  repeated titles; one lean threshold everywhere; give-or-take on category scores.
- **Naming the pattern** (run 3): the weakest area in plain words with a number to
  aim for, the compression pattern (too sure at the top, too cautious in the middle),
  and a free "since last week"; a Coach sample on the Plus card.
- **First-run words** (runs 1, 2): the ±5 starting point, "Guesser" (or "Getting
  started"), a plain read on a no-lean warm-up, "it isn't about being right" on the
  result, and when the first label could come.
