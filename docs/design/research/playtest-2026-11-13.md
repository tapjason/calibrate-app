# Blind playtest, 2026-11-13: days, not minutes

Raw findings, 2026-11-13. This is the eighteenth round. It ran over days, not one sitting:
- a diary across three sittings with the clock moved;
- a traveller whose time zone changed mid-run;
- a phone set to Arabic;
- a dropped connection.

Measures: expected against actual ease, how sure people were of what they understood, trust,
and UMUX-Lite. Eight people new to rounds 16–17. The plan, cast and criteria are in
[`round-plans.md`](round-plans.md#round-18).

These are what the testers saw, checked against the code; they are not decisions. **AI agents
playing people:** the numbers compare tasks and people. They aren't benchmarks against real users.
Where they differ, `CLAUDE.md`, `DESIGN_SYSTEM.md` and `UI_ROADMAP.md` win.

## Method

- **Build:** `builder/step-173-owner-picks` at `751bc3a` (step 173 and its reviews, pushed). Fixes
  made during the round were committed but not served until after the last run.
- **Health check:** the main session ran it before the round:
  - step 173 on the web build: Today, Insights, the card, Log's batch save and kept draft;
  - each stand-in: CDP time zone and locale, Playwright's clock, offline.
  - The browser defaults to `en-US` and Chicago time, so every earlier round saw US units and US
    dates. This round sets each person's locale.
- **New this round:**
  - **A diary run (Bea).** Three sittings: Friday evening (fresh install), Saturday evening, and
    the following Saturday. The same agent continued after the main session moved the clock.
  - **A traveller (Sanjana).** Part 1 in Kathmandu, part 2 in New York the next day, on a fresh tab
    with the new time zone.
  - **Locales:** ar-SA (Amira), en-GB (Bea), en-NZ (Kiri), en-NP (Sanjana), en-US (Carol, Kai).
  - **Offline (Carol):** the connection dropped right after the app loaded, for the whole run.
- **Measures:**
  - `Expect` before each task, SEQ after it;
  - each comprehension answer with "How sure: 50–100%";
  - Trust (three items, 1–5);
  - UMUX-Lite (two items, 1–7);
  - errors and first clicks;
  - mood at four moments;
  - best and worst moment;
  - a score out of 10.
- **Bugs fixed during the round**, committed after the runs that found them:
  - `3a8cd9a`: step 173's swallowed category tap; the answer run's scroll.
  - `a85c95f`: five copy and tap fixes. Not yet seen in the web build: the two in `3a8cd9a`.

## How sure of what they understood

There were 24 comprehension answers. People were on average **80% sure and 96% right**: 23 of 24
right, the 24th half right. Testers understood the screens better than they felt they did.

The three answers given at 65% or less mark where the app leaves people unsure:
- the card's marks (Walt 60%, guessing from the chart; Kiri 55%, didn't notice them);
- whose clock a reminder uses after travelling (Sanjana 65%).

Nobody was confidently wrong.

## What held up

- **Answering:** two taps, the same words for Yes and No, "one that doesn't isn't a mistake". It
  never once felt like a telling-off.
- **The person's own record on Log.** Marcus dropped 90 to 70 on "Answered 22 times before:
  happened 16 of 22." Walt's 20–40% line and Kai's 80–100% line did the same.
- **Coming back:** Bea after a week and Sanjana after three got no guilt, a Welcome back, and
  "Answer all".
- **Offline:** Carol lost her connection for the whole run and lost nothing.
- **"Times in 10":** loved by Bea, Carol, Amira, Sanjana and Kiri.
- **Kept draft, batch save, streak switch:** step 173 delivered on these.

## Ranked findings

### P1

1. **Privacy on a shared phone** (runs 8, 3).
   - Titles sit in full on Today and History, with no hide or lock. Before Sanjana answered,
     Today showed "Ana replies to my message today" in large type.
   - Nothing says what a due-day reminder shows on the lock screen.
   - Sanjana's only remedy was turning reminders off, which breaks the app.
   - Bea: Ollie can flip any answer within 24 hours, with no trace of who did.
   - D52 (3) closed this ("privacy beyond that isn't a concern"). A teenager on a family phone
     is new evidence. **Needs the owner** (aggregate B9).
2. **The privacy policy link opens "Site not found"** (run 8).
   - GitHub Pages isn't on yet, so it 404s, and it was the one place she went for an answer.
   - **Needs the owner** (HANDOFF: republish once Pages is on).
3. **Step 173's starter ideas swallowed the first category tap** (runs 3, 6).
   - Leaving the title field hid the ideas. The tap on a category is what leaves it, so the jump
     came as the tap landed.
   - **Fixed** (`3a8cd9a`): the ideas step aside once a category is chosen.
4. **The card at large text** (run 1).
   - The canvas shrinks to the window, so the key and chips were about 4–6 px at the 195 stand-in.
     There's no bigger view, and nothing outside the card explains the marks.
   - On an iPhone at AX5 the canvas keeps the phone's width, so it's less severe. The key is still
     12 pt scaled down.
   - Sanjana and Kiri found the strip meaningless.
   - **Needs the owner:** the marks explained beside the card in normal text, or tap to enlarge.
5. **A correction can turn a true answer into a lie that raises the score, and nothing counts
   it** (run 2). Within D34's 24 hours, Kai flipped a Yes to No: 85 → 86 and Forecaster.
   - **Needs the owner:** count corrections on Insights as deletions and late moves are, or
     accept it.
6. **Deleting an open prediction before its due day is free, while moving it is counted**
   (run 2). "I'll just delete my losers early."
   - This joins step 173's open question about deletes on the eve.
   - **Needs the owner.**

### P2

7. **"Is my number good?" and the Brier line** (runs 1, 2, 4).
   - The points line explains the number; nothing says good or not.
   - "Would score about the same … could be luck" and "said less than your base rate" read as a
     put-down three times.
   - **Needs the owner** (B5).
8. **Tier words beside a person's own sensitive category** (run 4).
   - "Getting started in Recovery" and "To reach Tracker in Recovery: 13 more scored answers"
     appear on Insights, the recaps and the card's VoiceOver summary. To someone eight months
     sober, that's a verdict on his recovery.
   - The card's visible face follows D27.
   - **Needs the owner.**
9. **Reminders** (runs 6, 5, 8; round 17 run 6).
   - One time of day, on the due day only: Carol's 7 pm came after her weigh-in.
   - Every default assumes a day schedule: 7 pm handover, practice picker 09:00, "With coffee
     8 AM".
   - Nothing says whose clock after travelling.
   - A prediction's details never say when it will remind.
   - **Needs the owner.**
10. **Today's verdict greets every open,** including right after a miss and on coming back
    (runs 4, 8). "You're overconfident at 80–100%" is the first sentence. **Needs the owner.**
11. **Nothing worth sending before 20** (runs 3, 7).

13. **The coverage note said "logged" but counts scored answers** (runs 3, 5). A 30% call logged a

16. **The streak row did nothing when tapped** (runs 4, 7). It was the first tap for two
    streak-wary people. **Fixed** (`a85c95f`): it opens How scoring works at Your streak.
17. **Switched-off reminder rows still named a day and time** (run 7). **Fixed** (`a85c95f`).
18. **Edit's late-move note didn't say it leaves the score alone** (run 2). **Fixed**
    (`a85c95f`).
19. **US units in brackets after metric** (run 6). "Written for somebody else first."
    **Needs the owner** (a D59 tweak: lead with US units on a US-region phone).
20. **Arabic dates and numerals inside English sentences** (run 5): "due الجمعة، ١٣ نوفمبر".
    Amira liked them. The mix with Western percentages and "05:00 PM" boxes is the question.

21. **Voice Control** (run 8).

    - Labels differ from what's shown ("In a week" is "Due in a week", "Work" is "Category: Work",

22. **No weather, family or study category** (runs 1, 3, 7). This is the fifth round running.
    **Needs the owner** (B4).
23. **An answered prediction's category is locked after 24 hours** (run 5). "Punished for a filing
    mistake." **Needs the owner** (D34).
