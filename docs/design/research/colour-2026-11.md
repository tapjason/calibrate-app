# Colour: should the app carry more of it?

Method: the main session (Fable) evaluated the UI and designed the options; three Opus
agents did scoped research (streak and reward visuals in popular apps; colour systems in
apps whose colour already means something, and in monochrome brands; a measurement of which
hues can sit beside the data colours). No code changed.

## 1. The short answer

1. **The streak's problem is form, not hue.** The flame is the same 18 pt ink icon before
   and after today counts, and the three goal dots carry no label. An outline that fills in,
   a larger count and a labelled goal fix that with no new colour.
2. **A classic orange flame is out.** It is the same orange as `over` to every viewer,
   normal vision included (CIEDE2000 5.3 from the `over` mark in both appearances), and both
   sit on Today.
3. **If a colour is wanted, raspberry is the only family that passes** against `over`,
   `under`, `calibrated`, `oracleGold` and `destructive` in both appearances, and it passes
   by 0.0 to 1.7. It would get one meaning: what the person did (the streak, a checkpoint, a
   badge earned), never how accurate they were.
4. **No published experiment isolates a streak's colour.** The published gains are from
   streak rules and from milestone moments (Duolingo, +1.7% day-7 retention for new
   learners). The milestone moment is planned and not built (`../FUTURE_UI.md` B1).

## 2. The UI as it stands (build 3)

What works:

- **Every hue is information.** On Today the one colour is the lean dot; on Insights the
  chart is where the colour budget goes. Nothing decorative competes with it.
- **It doesn't look like its category.** Polymarket is blue, Manifold and Fatebook indigo,
  Metaculus navy. Ink sets Fairly Sure apart from all of them and matches the icon and the
  launch page.
- **The honesty rules are visible:** no green or red for outcomes, no red for a missed
  streak.

What is weak:

- **State changes are under-signalled.** The streak row changes only its words when today
  counts. A checkpoint day is a grey tint. A badge earned is a card of text.
- **Long grey stacks.** Under Insights' rating, six lines of the same weight and colour.
  That is a hierarchy problem and colour would not fix it.
- **Today in dark is close to flat:** three bordered cards of equal weight (answers,
  streak, sign-in nudge), separated only by hairlines.

So the owner's instinct is right about the streak and about reward moments. It doesn't
follow that the chrome needs a hue.

Patterns across the 15 data-colour apps surveyed: green means good, up or yes in 7; the
brand hue shares a family with a data colour in 3; chrome is neutral or near-neutral in at
least 7. Of 14 monochrome or near-monochrome brands, 4 are hue-less, 5 use one accent, and 6
get their character from illustration, a mascot or 3D rather than a palette.

The costs of loud streaks are documented: a 2026 survey of 300 people aged 13–21 found 42%
would remove Snapstreaks if they could (2P, [EDRi](https://edri.org/our-work/new-study-reveals-how-young-people-are-influenced-by-gamification-features-on-snapchat/));
Headspace lets people hide the run streak, as this app does (D59).

Apple's guidance (HIG Color and Branding, updated 2025-12 and 2026-09) allows one accent
("in apps with primarily monochromatic content or backgrounds, choosing your brand color as
the app accent color can be an effective way to tailor your app experience") and says to
"avoid using the same color to mean different things" and to use an accent "intentionally
for primary actions or status indicators".

## 4. The measurement

For ten hue families, a grid search in OKLCH (hue 1°, lightness 0.005, chroma from 0.10) for
the best light and dark value that reads as a graphic (3:1 on `surface` and `canvas`) and as
text (4.5:1), scored by its smallest distance from the mark and text variants of `over`,
`under` and `calibrated`, plus `oracleGold` and `destructive`, under normal vision and
simulated protanopia, deuteranopia and tritanopia (Machado, Oliveira and Fernandes 2009,
severity 1.0, the dataviz palette validator's own matrices). Two metrics: CIEDE2000, pass
at 11; and the validator's OKLab ×100, pass at 8 colour-blind and 15 normal. The "worst
colour-blind ΔE 11.0" in `DESIGN_SYSTEM.md` §2.3 is the OKLab figure, not CIEDE2000.

|---|---|---|---|---|
| Raspberry | `#900068` | `#D806A1` icon, `#AE00C8` fill | 11.0–12.7 | Passes. Light as icon, text or a white-text fill. Dark as an icon or a white-text fill; **no dark text variant passes** (best misses by 0.1). |
| Indigo | `#4208D8` | `#48649F` | 6.9 | Light only. In dark it merges with `under` for every colour-blind viewer. |
| Coral | `#89292F` | `#9E4C4F` | 8.2 | Light only, and close to `destructive`. |
| Violet | `#740BFE` | `#7B5894` | 9.0 | Misses by 0.1 in light; merges with `under` for protan and deutan viewers. |
| Lime | `#7B9650` | `#526F10` | 7.5 | Merges with `calibrated` and `over` for protan and deutan viewers. |
| Flame orange | `#EF5E02` | `#FE6A00` | 5.3 | The classic flame. Same as the `over` mark for everyone; `oracleGold` for protan viewers in light (2.1); `destructive` for tritan viewers in dark (2.9). The best-separated orange is a brown (`#915824`, dark only). |
| Teal | `#087E9B` | `#01889B` | 5.6 | Merges with `under`. |
| Vermilion | `#961308` | `#B03F31` | 4.9 | Merges with `destructive` and `over`. |
| Gold | `#A37234` | `#8C5C1A` | 4.1 | Merges with `oracleGold` and `over`. |
| Amber | `#8C893B` | `#73650D` | 3.6 | Merges with `over` and gold; at 3:1 on white it is olive. |

Leaving `destructive` out (it appears only in You) moves flame orange from sixth to second
and it still fails against `over`.

Raspberry's margins (CIEDE2000 / colour-blind OKLab / normal OKLab, over the pass line):
light `#900068` +1.7 / +0.2 / +2.4; dark icon `#D806A1` +0.0 / +0.7 / +6.0; dark fill
`#AE00C8` with white text (5.78:1) +0.5 / +2.2 / +13.8. A dark fill with ink text passes
only at chroma 0.13 and a margin of 0.0, so a raspberry button in dark means white text on
a darker fill, unlike today's light fills with ink on them. No two-stop raspberry gradient
25–40° apart passes in light.

**Warm neutrals pass.** Two sets at today's lightness with a warm tint keep every contrast
`theme.test.ts` checks, in both appearances. Warm paper: light `canvas` `#F8F3EB`, `surface`
`#FFFDF9`, `surfaceSunken` `#F1EAE0`, `hairline` `#E6DFD5`, `controlBorder` `#90867C`, text
`#1B1511` / `#564D45` / `#71665D`; dark `#110F0C`, `#1C1915`, `#28231E`, `#342F29`,
`#797169`, text `#F6F3EC` / `#BEB8AD` / `#979085`.

**Found on the way, in today's palette** (not caused by anything proposed here):

- `over` and `oracleGold` are 2.1 CIEDE2000 apart for deutan viewers, and `over` and
  `destructive` 2.6. Each pair is told apart by shape and place (a hairline on an emblem; a
  delete button in You), which rule 0.5 requires, so nothing is broken; the warm region is
  simply full.
- In light, three pairs no test checks sit under their bar on `surfaceSunken`:
  `controlBorder` 2.93:1, the `calibrated` mark 2.86:1, `over` text 4.26:1.
- `DESIGN_SYSTEM.md` §2.5 says dark marks are "≥ 4.79:1 even on dark `surfaceSunken`"; `over`
  measures 4.65 and `under` 4.78. Both clear 3:1.

## 5. The options

1. **As shipped.**
2. **A clearer streak, still ink (recommended).** The flame is an outline until today
   counts, then filled; the count is larger; the goal dots are labelled. No rule changes.
3. **Raspberry for showing up.** Option 2, with the filled flame, the filled goal dots and
   the checkpoint tint in raspberry, and the same colour on a badge earned and a checkpoint
   moment. Graphic only: the count stays ink, because no dark text variant passes. Chrome
   stays ink. It adds a fourth meaning to rule 0.6 and a line to §2. A streak that ended is
   never coloured.
4. **Raspberry as the brand colour.** Actions, the selected tab and the "+" too. Reverses
   D33 seventeen days after it was decided; the icon and the launch page are ink; dark
   buttons change pattern. HIG allows it. Nothing measured says it helps.
5. **A classic orange flame.** Fails section 4.
6. **Warm paper neutrals.** Passes; a matter of taste. Warm cream is a common look in new
   apps this year, and the cool graphite matches the icon.

Later, not now: **selectable themes** as a Plus extra. Only the neutral set can vary safely
(graphite, paper, charcoal); an accent picker can't, since one hue family passes.

## 6. Recommendation

Build option 2 and the checkpoint moment (FUTURE_UI B1). Offer option 3 to the owner as the
one safe way to add colour, to be judged on his iPhone in both appearances before it is
built, because the margins are inside what a display or a different simulation could move.
Not 4, 5 or 6 before launch. If the answer to colour is ever yes, it should land before the
public release (`DESIGN_SYSTEM.md` §1, "Don't move the furniture after launch").

- Whether `#900068` reads as raspberry or as plum at 20 pt, and whether a magenta flame
  reads as a flame.
- The pass line of CIEDE2000 11 is this study's; the project's own gates are the OKLab ones.
- Hex values for Duolingo, Snapchat and Headspace aren't published; the Headspace, Finch and
  BeReal help pages were read from search snippets (403).
