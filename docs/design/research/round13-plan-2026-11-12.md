## Common blocks (in every brief)

**A. What you know** (the App Store page, word for word):

> **Fairly Sure** · Know when to trust your gut · Productivity · Free, offers in-app purchases
>
> Log a prediction, say how sure you are, and find out if you were right to be. Sharp in
> health, shaky with money? Your calibration score shows you where.
>
> Most people are overconfident about some things and underconfident about others, and
> never find out which. Fairly Sure shows you.
>
> HOW IT WORKS. Write a prediction ("I'll finish the report by Friday"), set how confident
> you are, from 0 to 100%, and pick a due date. As you set it, you see how your past calls
> at that confidence turned out. When it comes due, Fairly Sure reminds you. Tap yes or
> no. That's it.
>
> YOUR RESULT IN 60 SECONDS. Don't want to wait weeks? The warmup quiz gives you a first
> read on day one: ten quick either-or questions, each with how sure you are, and how
> your confidence compared with how many you got right.

**B.** `docs/playtest-appeal.md`, "For every brief", word for word.

**C. Rules.** http://localhost:8097. Never sign in, never buy, never erase
data, stay on localhost. Screenshots
`.playwright-mcp/playtest-2026-11-12b/run<n>-<moment>.png`. Say what you expect
before each meaningful tap and whether it happened; rate each task's ease 1–7
and each issue's severity 0–4. Answer the three end questions from memory.

## Success criteria (for synthesis only, never in a brief)

- Five-second read: says it's about how sure you are / whether your gut is
  right, not a to-do list or a quiz app.
- The Warm-up's result reached by every run; L1–L2 runs don't leave before it.
- A first real prediction logged from their own life, in under a minute.
- The share task: finds the card, can say what it shows; for a fresh install,
  the Day-0 card ("5 of 10 right. I was 77% sure. How sure are you?").
- Keep or pay: finds Plus, can say what it adds (the Coach, trends, cosmetics)
  and that the core is free.
- Comprehension: the score is about confidence matching outcomes, not being
  right; nothing counts until predictions are answered; 20 answers before a
  rating.

**End questions.** 1. What is the app for, in your own words? 2. What did the
