# Blind playtest, 2026-11-12: UI stress

Raw findings, 2026-11-12. Eight blind runs on the web build, all by personas
never cast before, plus a `web-playtester` stress pass and the main session's
clock, time-zone, CPU and offline stand-ins, run to the plan in
[`round-plans.md`](round-plans.md#round-12). The build is `builder/playtest-setup` (`ec61cd5`,
the first round on the playtest profiles); nothing in the app changed during
the round. These are what the testers saw, checked against the code; they are
not decisions. Where they differ, `CLAUDE.md`, `DESIGN_SYSTEM.md` and
`UI_ROADMAP.md` win.

## Method

**Why this round.** On the owner's iPhone 17, Log's title row and its × were
"sometimes" under the Dynamic Island (fixed the same day, `1851d9d`), and the
owner asked for rounds that stress the UI. So the cast and the checks push the
edges: the biggest and smallest phones, large text and high contrast, a
crowded list, long and non-Latin text, four-digit counts, impatient tapping, a
slow phone offline, a stylus, Voice Control and a tremor.

**The stress pass.** `web-playtester` covered every main screen and sheet on
the `stress` and `years` profiles and a fresh install, at 320 × 568, 402 × 874
and 568 × 320, light, zoom 1 and 2, with the impatient checks (double Save,
double Yes, browser back). It skipped 440 × 956, dark beyond the share card,
zoom 2 at 402, the Scoring page and an Answer-all run (its report lists every
skipped cell). It has no `browser_run_code_unsafe`, so **the main session ran
the stand-ins** afterwards: CPU throttled 4×, Pacific/Auckland and
America/Los_Angeles, a clock installed at 23:55 and fast-forwarded past
midnight, and offline after load. The skill now says so.

**Landscape is web-only.** The app is portrait only on iOS (`app.json`
`orientation`). The cast first had Lin in landscape; her run moved to
portrait before it started, and the stress pass's landscape shots are set
aside. app-profile §9 and §10 and the skill no longer list landscape.

**The cast** (plan, "The cast"): nobody from the last two rounds; seven of the
ten personas added today, plus the wildcard.

## What held up

- **No doubles.** Double-clicking Save, Yes, Answer it now and Done never made
  a second prediction or answer (run 2, stress pass).
- **Offline.** Logging, answering, Answer all, changing an answer and every
  screen worked with no connection; Plus said "Plus plans couldn't load. Check
  your connection, then open this again." with no crash (run 6, stand-in).
- **Midnight.** A Log opened at 23:55 and saved at 00:05 re-dated itself
  ("Due Friday, Nov 13" to "Due Saturday, Nov 14") and saved due the day after
  the save, so D41 holds across the boundary.
- **Time zones.** Auckland and Los Angeles both load; due days shift with the
  zone as noon-stored dates should.
- **Scripts and counts.** Arabic, Japanese, Ge'ez, Hindi, ZWJ emoji, a 60-letter
  word and a pasted link all rendered whole; "1,001-day streak", "1,000
  scored" and the 1,000-answer card fit at 320.
- **The words that worked:** "about 7 times in 10"; "Save, due Sunday"; "To
  save, add a category and a confidence"; "Your first answer: tomorrow evening";
  "The number you said stays as it is: it's the record."; "Furthest off:
  Finance… About 50% would have matched."; Show the counts as a chart for the
  ear; the Welcome back card; "All caught up: 6 answered, 4 happened, you
  expected 4 or 5"; the unlock moment; "a grey dot has fewer than 10 answers".
- **The track record while setting a number** changed numbers in runs 3, 5, 6
  and 8.
- **Console:** no app errors across the round.

## Ranked findings

### P1: breaks and record risks

1. **The way out scrolls away on three sheets.** Log's header rides inside its
   scroll view (`app/log.tsx`, on purpose since the 2026-11-07 formSheet bug),
   Share's × sits in its scroll content (`app/share/index.tsx:45-62`), and How
   scoring works puts Close inside `ScoringExplainer` in the scroll; opened
   from the identity line it jumps to Badges, so Close starts 1,660 px (run 3)
   or 3,600 px (run 7) above. Categories, Practice and the details sheets keep
   theirs outside. At 320 × 568 Log's × is off screen after one scroll (stress
   pass). On iOS swipe-down is the fallback, which a stylus or Voice Control
   user can't do. Runs 3 and 7 (stylus, large text), stress pass. Severity 3.
   **Needs the owner:** pin all three (Log is a page sheet now, so the
   formSheet bug that moved its header doesn't apply), or open Scoring at the
   top.
2. **Voice Control can't set a confidence.** "−5" / "+5" are named "Lower /
   Raise confidence by 5"; the slider needs a drag; "tap Confidence" does
   nothing. Log's "+" is a bare symbol named "Log a prediction", and "Save,
   due tomorrow" is named "Save prediction, due tomorrow". Run 5 (Voice
   Control), stress pass. Severity 3. **No decision needed:** names that start
   with the visible words ("+5", "−5", "Save, due tomorrow", with the longer
   wording as the hint); the digest's "Sun" chips likewise.
3. **An answer changes by one stray tap, and the change isn't said where the
   eye is.** After "Change to: it didn't happen" the same button, in the same
   place, offers the opposite; the new status is above, out of view at large
   text, and a wrap moves the buttons about 20 px (runs 6, 7, 8). Run 2's
   double tap on Done after a Resolve opened from History went through to the
   reordered list and opened another prediction with "Change to" under the
   finger (web; on iOS the sheet's dismissal may swallow it, check on the
   phone). Resolve's result leads with "Recorded:", so a tremor's wrong No read
   as success (run 8). Severity 3. **Needs the owner:** say the change beside
   the button and hold the button a moment; whether the result leads with the
   outcome (Yes and No stay identical, R8).
4. **Rating lines disagree within minutes** (run 4): Today 89, then Resolve's
   "Your rating stays at 88", then 90, then 89 on the card. `ratingChangeLine`
   reads `shownRating()` before and after; the Answer-all run may read a stale
   "before". Not traced. Severity 1, but a record line. **No decision needed:**
   trace it.

### P1: the record's story

5. **"Am I good at guessing?" has no plain answer.** Five runs met the Today
   bar with "70 / 85 / 90" and no words (3, 4, 5, 6, 7; Lin read 85 as Sharp).
   The Brier line ("Saying 70% every time… would score 0.21") read as a put-down
   with nothing to do about it (3, 5, 6, 7, 8). Under "You're well calibrated"
   the expected-count subtitle read as a miss (7). The unlock screen says "From
   here the number means something" without the give-or-take Insights shows
   (3). **Needs the owner** (D24, D57, the hero's design).
6. **A category's own read is missing.** Insights' category rows don't open
   anything (runs 4, 7); no row says which way that category leans (3, 2); a
   repeated prediction's record ("my walks") is only on Log (7, 8). Viktor found
   range → History → chip on his own. **Needs the owner.**
7. **A shared phone has no privacy** (run 8, edge of the audience): no lock,
   titles on Today and in reminders; "Hide" only takes a category off Log.
   Aisling gave up. **Needs the owner** (a lock, or Face ID, is new scope; what
   reminders show on the lock screen is decided per notification).

### P2: the people the app wasn't shaped around

8. **Increase Contrast and Bold Text aren't read.** Nothing in `src/` or `app/`
   reads either setting, and the custom typeface may not take Bold Text. The
   faintest text is also the smallest ("2 rest days saved", "top badge
   reached", the range chips' "0–20%", the chart's n=). Run 7 (cataracts).
   **Needs the owner** (DESIGN_SYSTEM §2, §3).
9. **Things move under the finger.** Log's track-record line appears at the
   second +5 and pushes the due-date chips down about 46 px (runs 3, 8); the
   starter ideas vanish on the first letter of a first prediction, lifting the
   form about 300 px (run 1, `LogPredictionForm.tsx:297`); the Warm-up's intro
   goes after question 1 (run 1, by design for 667 pt phones); the delete
   confirmation puts Delete where Answer it now was (run 1). **No decision
   needed** for reserving the track-record line's height; the rest need the
   owner.
10. **Targets too close.** The Warm-up's Next and "Skip for now" touch, and
    Skip's `hitSlop={8}` reaches into Next on iOS (run 1,
    `WarmupQuiz.tsx:198-218`); Edit and Answer it now, Save changes and Cancel
    about 4 px apart (run 3); History rows flush (run 8); the digest's day chips
    3 px apart (run 5). **No decision needed:** a gap of `space.sm` in each.
11. **Categories' switches clip at the card's edge and crowd Rename** (runs 4,
    8 at 390 and 440 wide). The iOS switch is wider than the web one (63 × 28),
    so check on the phone. Hidden shows only as the switch: Viktor never knew
    Finance was hidden (run 4). **No decision needed** for the layout; a
    "Hidden" word needs the owner.
12. **Answer all.** Yes and No need a Next tap, Can't tell moves on by itself
    (runs 4, 6); at 320 a badge card on answer 4 of 6 pushed Next below the
    fold (run 6, and after a No, which read oddly); back leaves the run, and
    "All caught up" counts only what came after (run 4). **Needs the owner.**
13. **A long streak ends with no word about rest days** (run 6: 41 days, two
    rest days saved, nothing said about what they covered); the streak row
    doesn't open (runs 6, 7). **Needs the owner.**
14. **Drafts aren't kept.** Closing Log keeps nothing; iOS asks before
    discarding, the web doesn't (runs 1, 2, 8, all interrupted mid-typing).
    **Needs the owner:** keep a draft for a while.
15. **"Erase all data on this device"** is the last row on You, just under
    Terms of use (run 8, tremor). Alone at the bottom by design
    (`SettingsView.tsx:45`); it asks before erasing. **Needs the owner** if it
    should move further.

### P2: words and formats

16. Dates are month first and times 12-hour whatever the region (runs 5, 8),
    and You shows "19:00" in the field under "7:00 PM" (runs 1, 5): the eighth
    round's finding 7 (`expo-localization`), still open.
17. "On its way" for an open prediction (runs 1, 2, 3); "Until 2:16 AM
    tomorrow" for the 24-hour window (2, 6, 7, 8); "1001 answered" in History
    beside "1,001 scored" (7, **no decision needed**); "Logged again, due …" on
    a two-week-old answer, or matching a same-titled card in another category
    (4, 8); "you ran well calibrated" (5); "Trends: your 4 months on file" after
    five months (5).

### P2: consistency and structure

18. **History's category chips** run off the right edge with no cue, and
    tapping one slides the strip so "All" goes off the left (runs 2, 4, 5, 6,
    7). **Needs the owner** (wrap, or a cue).
19. **The chart's n= labels** sit on the dots and run together at big counts
    ("n=286n=287") (stress pass, runs 7, 8). **No decision needed.**
20. **A long reflection shows in full** inside History's list card, about
    200 px of text mid-list (run 4). **No decision needed:** clamp it to a few
    lines there.
21. **Content scrolls under the floating ×** on Resolve's result and the
    details sheet, with no bar behind it (run 7). **No decision needed.**
22. **The card starts with every category on,** including ones hidden from
    Log and others' (run 4: thirteen labels about 9 px tall). The card's own
    toggles are separate from Log's hiding (`ShareCardPanel.tsx:66`). **Needs
    the owner.**
23. **No monthly look.** Share has a week and a year; Trends is Plus (run 5).
    **Needs the owner.**

### P3: polish and measures

24. At CPU 4×: Today in 5.0 s from cold, Log ready in 2.0 s, typing about
    130 ms a key (4.1 s for 32 characters against a 1.0 s floor).
25. With 1,001 answers, Resolve's buttons show "…" for 1–2 s after a tap (run
    7): the recompute over the whole record. Measure on the phone.
26. The "+" to log is 32 px (44 with `hitSlop` on iOS) and top-right; Olu
    counted every top-corner stretch (runs 1, 3, 5).
27. "On these ten, no clear lean" after 9 of 10 read as a shrug (run 1; D36's
    rule, as decided); Insights is empty on day one (run 1).
28. The track-record line the App Store text promises "as you set it" waits
    for 5 answers in the range (run 2; D57 (a), open).
