# Round 15 plan, 2026-11-12: appeal, staying power (the follow-up)

The third appeal round of the day, and the last before aggregating rounds
12–15 (owner, 2026-11-12). Rounds 13 and 14 were mostly the first five
minutes, and the clearest split in both was **having a record**: every fresh
install but one was "not disappointed", every run with a record "somewhat" or
"very". Five round-13 people said "ask me again after Saturday / Wednesday /
a month". This round asks them, on the playtest profiles that put them
there, plus a wildcard on the longest record. Same build, same kit. Findings
go to `playtest-2026-11-12d.md`.

needs them; this focus is them. Each brief says what they did and felt last
time, in their terms, so the run tests whether the app kept its promise.

runs (two rounds did those); no one under 26 (Zara and Haruto did the
