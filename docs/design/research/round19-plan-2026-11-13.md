## What changes from round 18

Round 18's diary run (Bea) said the most about lasting use: kind at every return, but by week two
there was nothing to care about before 20 answers. This round leans on that:

- **Four diary runs, on four rhythms.** The main session moves the clock between sittings; the
  same agent continues.
  - Grace: morning, next evening, then **15 days away**, past the welcome back's 14 days.
  - Yusuf: a night baker **across midnight**: 23:50, 00:20, then the next night.
  - Priya: Sunday's check-in, a weekday, the next Sunday.
  - Hannah: ten-minute windows on days 0, 2 and 5, with Reduce Motion.
- **Four single sittings aimed at open decisions:**
  - Elena: privacy on a long record;
  - Rosa: sharing before 20 answers;
  - Tunde: a slow old phone on prepaid data;
  - the wildcard.

  Each starts with **three minutes and no task**, "use it the way you would", so we see what
  people do when nobody steers them.
- **Profiles that fit the person.** Round 18's profiles carried office and running titles for a
  retiree and a student. This round, five people start fresh and the three on a profile match its
  content (Priya and Elena on `long-time`; Tunde on `return`, framed as his evening classes).
- **Step 174 checked in passing:**
  - Grace's and Hannah's first category tap;
  - the streak row's tap;
  - the coverage note's words;
  - "Tracker or higher";
  - the answer run starting at the top.

## Measures (comparable with round 18, plus a curve)

- Kept from round 18:
  - `Expect` before and SEQ after each task;
  - "How sure: 50–100%" on each comprehension answer;
  - Trust (three items);
  - UMUX-Lite (two items);
  - errors and first clicks;
  - mood at four moments;
  - best and worst moment;
  - a score out of 10.
- **New for diaries:** at the end of every sitting, `Tomorrow: <0–10>` (how likely to open it
  tomorrow) and one word for how it felt. This gives a retention curve across sittings.
- **New for single sittings:** the first three minutes without a task. Record what was tapped, in
  order, and what they made of it.
