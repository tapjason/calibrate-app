# Blind playtest, 2026-11-12 (c): appeal, second cast

Raw findings, 2026-11-12. The second appeal round, run straight after the
thirteenth (`playtest-2026-11-12b.md`) on the same build
(`builder/playtest-setup`, app unchanged since `6e6e28d`), with a new cast and
the same kit (`docs/playtest-appeal.md`). Plan: [`round-plans.md`](round-plans.md#round-14).
These are what the testers saw, checked against the code; they are not
decisions. **The testers are AI agents playing people:** the appeal answers
show where groups part ways, never a market number. Where they differ,
`CLAUDE.md`, `DESIGN_SYSTEM.md` and `UI_ROADMAP.md` win.

## Method

The cast, the wildcard's draw and the checks against §10's appeal rules are in
the plan. In short: Teresa (L1, 70, Quezon City), Linda (L2, 74, Dayton),

main session cleared the stored settings, so nothing carried over (round 13
finding 24). Interruptions in runs 1, 5 and 8.

## What held up

The same as round 13, confirmed: "about 7 times in 10" (every run), "Save, due
Sunday" (runs 1, 2, 3, 5, 8), honesty, the answer key's facts (runs 1, 2, 5,
8), the paywall's plainness (runs 1, 2, 4, 7, 8). New this round:
- **Answer all and the welcome back** (runs 4, 6): "All caught up. 3 answered.
  1 happened. You expected 1 or 2." was Sophie's best moment; the welcome back
  "told me exactly what was waiting… with no guilt-trip" (Haruto).
- **The track record and the title match on Log** moved Haruto's number from
  75 to 65 and showed Ingrid "Answered once before: it didn't happen" without
  the old number.
- **The after-answer line:** "one that doesn't isn't a mistake, and one that
  does isn't proof" (Ingrid's +2); "the pattern over many is what counts"
  (Sophie).
- **Correcting an answer** in three taps inside the 24 hours (Haruto).
- **The daily practice** is Linda's reason to come back tomorrow and part of
  Teresa's.
- **No account, no permission ask, "live only on this phone"** (Nusrat:
  "exactly the sentence I wanted").

## Ranked findings

Round 13's numbering is kept where a finding repeats ("R13 #2"). New
findings first within each group.

### P1: the record

1. **"Can't tell" lets the misses wait** (run 7, L5). A skip can be answered
   for a week (D45), so a person can skip everything and answer only what
   happened; Insights counts "Can't tell" beside the rating only once it's
   unlocked. **Needs the owner** (D45: show the count from the first answer;
   or count a skip that turns into a Yes). Run `record-auditor` with R13 #1
   (moving a due date).
2. **The unlock estimate jumps** 12 days on one answer ("about 6 weeks (around
   Dec 22)" to "about 4 weeks (around Dec 10)") and carries no range, unlike
   every other number (run 7). **Needs the owner** (D53).
3. **Sure things still buy a badge** (run 5: "ten of those a day and I'd have
   50 in about a week"; it made him distrust his friend's card). D41 says it
   raises the cost and doesn't end it; recorded as the competitive user's
   read. **Needs the owner** only to revisit.

### P1: lost before the first reward (R13 #2–#4 again)

4. **The Warm-up's first question and the practice decks read as school.**
   R13 #2, now named by most fresh installs across both rounds; this round Teresa, Ramón, Nusrat (−1, 0, −1 at the first screen),
   and the day's practice opened on atomic numbers again for Linda, Sophie
   and Nusrat ("Plutonium or Radon" at 10 pm and 1 am). US-centric practice
   (Chicago or Washington) for Haruto and Ingrid. **Needs the owner.**
5. **"No clear lean" is the honest answer and the boring one** (runs 3, 5, 8;
   R13 #4). People who came for a verdict (a Reel, a friend's 91) got "the
   app shrugged". The result's long English beneath (the thesis, the chart
   caption) lost Teresa and Linda. "About 4 in 10 of them shouldn't" has no
   verb after "shouldn't" for Linda, an English teacher. **Needs the owner.**
6. **The share squares are never explained** (run 6; R13 run 5). A friend's
   blue square couldn't be read, and posting his own orange told his group
   "too sure" before the app had told him. Neither the card nor the text
   share has a legend. **Needs the owner** (a one-line key in the text share,
   or on the card).

### P1: no reason to come back

7. **Nothing to compare against** (run 5, R13 run 6): someone who arrives from
   a friend's challenge finds no way to answer it. Dev would pay only for
   this (₹300–500 once, a friends board). New scope. **Needs the owner.**
8. **The daily practice is the habit, and it's walled off** (runs 2, 8): not
   shareable ("3 of 3" is the Wordle habit between Linda and her
   granddaughter) and outside the streak ("feels backwards"; "makes practice
   feel pointless" to a Duolingo user). Decided (CLAUDE.md §9, D17).
   **Needs the owner** to revisit, at least a share for the day's result.
9. **Twenty answers, months away** (runs 1, 2, 3, 5, 8; R13 #5); Ramón: "for
   Friday nights that's 20 weeks"; he'd stay for "your busy-night guesses: 3
   of 4 happened" after a week. **Needs the owner.**
10. **Built for an office** (runs 1, 2, 3, 5, 6; R13 #6): no Family, Weather,
    Sport, Study or business; Ramón found You → Categories only by digging,
    and Dev's friend's card itself said "Forecaster in sport". **Needs the
    owner** (an "Add your own" chip on Log).

### P2: trust and privacy

11. **What a reminder shows on the lock screen isn't said, and it's the
    words** (run 8, a 16-year-old sharing a room; round 12's finding 7). The
    due-day reminder's body is the prediction's title (`scheduler.ts:315`),
    on by default at 7 PM; You doesn't say so and offers no way to hide it.
    Nusrat would turn notifications off and then forget to answer. **Needs the
    owner** (a "show the words" switch, or neutral text; say it in You).
12. **Usage stats on by default** (run 7, EU sensibility), sent only after
    sign-in. **Needs the owner**; `policy-auditor`.

### P2: tone and identity

13. **"Overconfident" five times in one evening** (run 4): Today's headline,
    Insights' title, both recaps and the Plus sample. "Each time it's fair,
    but… it feels like nagging." And Insights opens on a block of grey
    statistics above the chart (give or take, "within its give-or-take",
    Brier, "No answers were Can't tell", "at its low end"); the kind sentence
    ("It isn't about being right") is only in How scoring works. Runs 4, 6;
    R13 #10. **Needs the owner.**
14. **A rating that rises after a miss, unexplained** (run 4: 87 to 88 after a
    70% No), and **a badge right after a mis-tapped No** (run 6). Both are
    right (a miss at 70% can move a range toward its number; Yes and No get
    identical feedback, R8), but read as "did I do it wrong?". **Needs the
    owner** (a line on why).
15. **✕ beside "it didn't happen"** reads as a teacher's mark (run 4), in
    Resolve and History, against ✓ for Yes. R8 is about feedback; this is the
    status mark. **Needs the owner.**
16. **The week recap is a bright card of numbers at night** (run 4: "Surest
    thing that didn't"), and "Boldest call that landed" reads as a game show
    (runs 6, 7; R13 #21). **Needs the owner.**
17. **The same-day rule loses genuine forecasts, and the reason is two screens
    away** (run 7: logged at 08:00 for an 18:00 club run). History's details
    say only "Answered the day it was logged, so it isn't scored." **No
    decision needed** for adding the reason there (D56's wording); **needs
    the owner** for any change to the rule (D41).

### P2: losing work and small breaks

18. **An interruption loses the half-typed prediction** (runs 1, 5; R13 run 2;
    round 12's finding 14). Teresa's −2, the round's deepest dip. On iOS an
    app switch keeps memory; a kill or a reload doesn't. **Needs the owner**
    (keep a draft).
19. **The Warm-up's current question loses its pick on return** (run 8; R13
    run 3): questions before it are kept. **No decision needed:** keep the
    pick and number with the progress.
20. **No sign a save worked** when Log was opened from Insights (runs 6, 7):
    the sheet closes back to Insights unchanged. **No decision needed** (the
    D48 scroll only runs on Today): a brief "Saved, due …" on the screen
    below.
21. **Warm-up question 1 under the fixed bar** at 375 × 812 with large text
    (runs 1, 2), not only at 320 (R13 #20); Log's "Due Sunday" line behind
    Save at the same size (runs 1, 2). **No decision needed.**
22. **The paywall's big "Start 1 month free" comes before "free forever"**
    (run 1: afraid her daughter's card would be charged). **Needs the owner**
    (order).
23. **"Down 1 since last week, from 4 answers"** for someone who wasn't there
    last week (run 6), and the week recap counting October predictions
    answered tonight as "this week". **Needs the owner.**
