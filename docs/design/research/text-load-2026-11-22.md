## Found along the way: a reminder promised before permission

An open prediction's details say "Due Mon, Nov 23, with a reminder at 7:00 PM" on a fresh install
where nobody has allowed notifications yet. `PredictionDetails.tsx:158` reads only the app's own
switches (`notificationsEnabled && remindersEnabled`, both on by default), not the iOS
permission. Until "Turn on reminders" is tapped and allowed, no reminder fires, so the line
promises one that won't come. It would hit a real iPhone too. The fix: read the permission
state, then say "reminders off" or nothing.
