# Round 17 plan, 2026-11-12: usability, measured differently

The owner (2026-11-12): after the round 16 fixes, run another round whose
criteria and combinations of factors differ a little from the last ones, so
it gives a sound reading of the user experience. Findings go to
`playtest-2026-11-12f.md`.

## What differs from rounds 13–16

**The measures.** Rounds 13–16 used the appeal kit: the UEQ-S pairs, reaction
words and the disappointment question. This round measures **usability and
task success** with standard instruments, so a different side of the
experience is scored:

- **SEQ** (Single Ease Question), after every task: "Overall, how easy or
  difficult was this task?" from 1 (very difficult) to 7 (very easy). The
  usual benchmark is about 5.5.
- **First click:** for each findability task, where the first tap went and
  whether it was on the path.
- **Errors and recoveries:** each wrong turn, mis-tap or dead end, and how it
  was recovered. Counted per task.
- **SUS** (System Usability Scale), the ten standard items, once at the end
  (scored 0–100; 68 is average).
- **Critical incidents:** the single best and worst moment, with the screen.
- **HEART at the end:**
  - Happiness: score out of 10.
  - Engagement: what they'd do tomorrow.
  - Adoption: would they log a second one unprompted.
  - Retention: back in a week?
  - Task success: kept for synthesis.

The mood line stays, at four moments only (first screen, first save, first
answer or result, the end), so the feeling stays comparable with earlier
rounds.

**The combinations.** Every persona is new to rounds 12–16, and each pairs
axes in ways no earlier run has:

- an access need with a start state it hasn't met;
- tech literacy against age, the "wrong" way round;
- a mindset against a stage.

## Common blocks (in every brief)

**A.** The App Store page, as in round 13.

**B. How it went** (new this round):
- After each task, write `SEQ: <1–7>` for "Overall, how easy or difficult was
  this task?" (1 very difficult, 7 very easy), then
  `Errors: <n> (<what>, how you got out)`.
- For each task where you had to find something, write `First click: <where>
  (on the path / off it)`.
- Mood (−3 to +3, a few words why) at the first screen, your first save,
  your first answer or the quiz result, and the end.
- At the end:
  ```
  SUS (1 strongly disagree – 5 strongly agree):
  1 I think I would like to use this app frequently: <n>
  2 I found the app unnecessarily complex: <n>
  3 I thought the app was easy to use: <n>
  4 I think I would need the support of a technical person to use this app: <n>
  5 I found the various functions in this app were well integrated: <n>
  6 I thought there was too much inconsistency in this app: <n>
  7 I imagine most people would learn to use this app very quickly: <n>
  8 I found the app very cumbersome to use: <n>
  9 I felt very confident using the app: <n>
  10 I needed to learn a lot of things before I could get going with this app: <n>
  Best moment: <what, where>
  Worst moment: <what, where>
  Tomorrow I would: <…>   A second prediction unprompted: <yes/no, why>
  Back in a week: <yes/maybe/no, why>   Score: <n>/10
  ```
