## What differs from rounds 13–17

**The situation.** Every earlier round was one sitting. The app's loop takes days: log tonight,
answer tomorrow, come back next week. This round adds the following:

- **A diary run.** One person plays over three sittings. The main session moves the browser's
  clock between them (Playwright's clock: the next evening, then a week later). The same agent
  continues, so they meet their own predictions coming due. It measures what no single sitting
  can: whether the reminder of what they said makes sense a day later, and whether coming back
  after a week is welcoming.
- **Real-world stand-ins set by the main session:**
  - a time zone (CDP `Emulation.setTimezoneOverride`) for the traveller;
  - a locale (`Emulation.setLocaleOverride`), so the US units show only for the US persona, and
    numbers and dates follow the person's region;
  - offline after load for the rural user.
- **Step 173 checked in passing.** The tasks lead people past the new things without naming them:
  - Today's rating tap;
  - the closest badge;
  - the keys;
  - "Save and log another";
  - the kept draft;
  - the streak switch;
  - a late due-date move;
  - US units.

**The measures.** Rounds 13–16 used the appeal kit; round 17 used SEQ and SUS. This round:

- **Expected and actual ease.** Before each task, "Expect: 1–7"; after it, "SEQ: 1–7"
  (Albert and Dixon's expectation measure). A task that came out much harder than expected is a
  surprise worth a finding even when its SEQ is middling.
- **How sure of what they understood.** Each comprehension answer comes with "How sure: 50–100%".
  This scores the app's own idea on the testers: are people as sure of what a screen means as
  they should be? Confident and wrong is the worst case, a screen that misleads.
- **Trust,** three items (1–5):
  - "I trust the numbers this app shows me";
  - "It treats me fairly";
  - "I'd change what I do because of it".
- **UMUX-Lite** in place of SUS (two items, 1–7):
  - "This app's capabilities meet my requirements";
  - "This app is easy to use".
- **Kept from round 17:** errors and first clicks, best and worst moments, a score out of 10,
  back in a week.
- **The mood line** at four moments only, so the feeling stays comparable.
