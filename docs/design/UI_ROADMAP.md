## 1. Where it stands

Every step through 187 is built and on `master`. Every decision in §2 except D5, D6, D11
and D12 is decided, and each that asked for a change in the app is built (D44 sets the
rules for social features after v1), with a pass over D23–D31 and an accessibility pass on
2026-10-12 (see [`BUILT_LOG.md`](BUILT_LOG.md) §1.5). **Everything not listed in the
parking lot or §2 is done.** What's left: the owner's checks on the iPhone (TestFlight
1.0.0 build 2, which carries steps through 186; checklist `docs/HUMAN_VERIFICATION.md`),
the open work in `docs/SHIP_READINESS.md` ("Open work") and the decisions below.

Verification for any new UI step: `bash scripts/check.sh`, a web-build screenshot at phone
width (320 and 375pt, both appearances; bring the automation browser's window to the front,
or force a repaint, before each capture: hidden behind other windows it keeps
presenting its first frame, the launch spinner, long after the screen has drawn), and an
iPhone run for anything with haptics, symbols, sheets or glass (web shows none of them).
An iPhone run means the development build on the owner's iPhone (since 2026-11-07) or a
TestFlight build (the App Store's Expo Go stops at SDK 54); the owner runs it, from the
list `/device-check` writes.

### Notes from the 2026-10-06 element research

- **Structure before launch, not after:** reviews punish a changed UI more than anything
  else in the sample (0.8% of five-star reviews, 6.1% of one- and two-star), and
  Chen et al. found 90% of "it used to look better" reviews are 1–2★. That is why the
  structural calls (navigation D3, the typeface D1 and D33, dark mode D7) were made
  before release, and why the layout should hold still after it (DESIGN_SYSTEM §1).
- **What stays:** labelled chips and pills, the slider with ±5, Share's segmented
  tabs and the bullet bar are what the top apps or the HIG would choose too
  (DESIGN_SYSTEM §7.20). The bar is a deliberate departure from the rings of WHOOP,
  Bevel and Oura: calibration bands are thresholds, not progress toward 100.

## 3. Reference apps

Best overall references, and what to take from each:

1. **(Not Boring) Habits** (ADA 2022, $29.99/yr and $59.99 lifetime when checked,
   paid skins) — the Resolve press → haptic → acknowledgement, and cosmetics as Plus.
2. **WHOOP / Oura** — one 0–100 score, named bands, a grey "calibrating" state; Oura's
   70/85 cut points match Fairly Sure's badge thresholds.
3. **Spotify Wrapped 2025 + Co–Star** — archetype + one real receipt + selective colour
   (500M shares on day one); blunt copy on a stark canvas.
4. **Gentler Streak** (ADA 2024) — no-guilt tone for "Getting started" and misses.
5. **Bevel** — free scores, paid AI interpretation: the closest analogue to Coach-as-Plus.
6. **Strava Year in Sport 2025** — counter-example: paywalling the recap drew public
   backlash, which backs "never paywall a shareable".

For how these and a dozen other 4.7+ apps choose individual controls (tabs, the add
action, sheets, settings rows, confirmations, the rating prompt), see
[`research/elements-2026-10.md`](research/elements-2026-10.md): Daylio and How We
Feel for logging in a few taps, Streaks and Todoist for Settings as a grouped list,
Oura for a short Liquid Glass tab bar with the add action beside it, and Clue as the
counter-example for upsells that interrupt.
